About me.

My work explores identity, memory, and the emotional weight of human experience. Rooted in personal history and cultural lineage, I use symbolism and layered narratives to reflect themes of love, guidance, loss, and resilience. Figures in my paintings often carry altered or symbolic forms—distorted, abstracted, or reimagined—to represent the complexity of inner worlds and inherited identity. Through color, repetition, and iconography, I translate emotions that are often difficult to articulate into visual language. Family, especially maternal influence, plays a central role in my work. I reflect on the presence of care, protection, and silent strength, while also confronting tension, absence, and generational impact. My compositions are intentional but intuitive, allowing space for both control and raw expression. Ultimately, my work invites viewers to reflect on their own experiences—where they come from, what has shaped them, and how memory continues to live within the present.
